**Module 7: Behavioral Finance Corner**

**The Cognitive Trap: Anchoring Bias** Risk managers often anchor internal credit loss models to the ultra-low default rates and cheap liquidity conditions of the 2010–2021 regime. This creates a false baseline, leading underwriters to treat high base rates as a temporary anomaly rather than the structural reality.

**Rule of Thumb:** Always stress-test debt service coverage models using historical peak base rates (e.g., 2006–2007 levels) rather than 5-year trailing averages to ensure balance sheet resilience.
