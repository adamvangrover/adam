### SECTION 3: DEEP-DIVE: CREDIT METRICS, LMTS, AND CLO ARBITRAGE

#### 1. EBITDA Add-Back Proliferation & The Coverage Deficit
Underwriting models throughout the 2021–2022 vintage routinely accepted EBITDA add-backs exceeding 25–40% of reported earnings for "run-rate synergies" and "restructuring adjustments." As these facilities hit their 4th year:
- **Realization Rate:** Less than **48%** of projected pro-forma add-backs have materialized as actual cash flow.
- **Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio (FCCR):** True median FCCR across middle-market BSL borrowers has contracted from 1.82x (in 2022) to **1.08x** in Q3 2026.
- **Debt Service Capacity Under SOFR @ 3.93%:** For a standard corporate with 5.5x debt-to-EBITDA, interest expense alone consumes **47.5%** of unadjusted EBITDA. When combined with maintenance CapEx and taxes, Cash Available for Debt Service (CADS) yields an effective DSCR of **0.94x**.

#### 2. The Rise of Aggressive Liability Management Transactions (LMTs)
Sponsors and distressed issuers are increasingly exploiting loose covenant-lite loan documents to execute coercive restructurings:
- **Up-Tiering ("Serta-style" Transactions):** Majority lenders (50.1%) cooperate with sponsors to create "super-priority" priming facilities, demoting minority lenders into structural subordination.
- **Drop-Down Asset Transfers ("J.Crew-style"):** Material intellectual property, foreign subsidiaries, or brand assets are transferred to "unrestricted subsidiaries" to serve as collateral for fresh high-yield liquidity, leaving existing BSL holders unsecured.
- **Double-Dip & Pari Plus Financing:** Exploiting intercompany loan baskets to assert multiple claims against the same operational assets. Institutional investors not actively monitoring credit agreement negative covenants risk instantaneous 30-40% recovery impairments.

#### 3. CLO Arbitrage & Technical Engine Drivers
Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs) represent **~68% of total US BSL purchasing demand**. The health of the CLO engine dictates secondary loan pricing:
- **Liability Spreads:** AAA CLO liability spreads have stabilized at **SOFR + 130 to +140 bps**, enabling managers to print new vehicles ($138B YTD issuance).
- **The Weighted Average Spread (WAS) Squeeze:** As primary BSL repricings compress asset yields to SOFR + 350 bps, while CCC baskets edge toward the 7.5% covenant cap, CLO equity net cash distributions have declined from 14.5% to **10.8% annualized**.
- **Maturity Wall Trigger:** Over $220 Billion of BSL loans mature in 2027–2028. If SOFR remains elevated above 3.50%, CLO managers will be unable to reinvest proceeds into lower-rated extension amendments without violating portfolio weighted average rating factor (WARF) tests.
