3. Credit Fundamentals, Liability Management & CLO Arbitrage

EBITDA ADD-BACK DEFICIT

Pro-forma EBITDA add-backs across 2021–2022 LBO vintages averaged 28% of reported earnings. Actual realization sits below 48%, driving true leverage 1.5x–2.0x higher than marketed and dropping median Cash Available for Debt Service (CADS) coverage to 0.94x.

LMT & PRIMING EPIDEMIC

Covenant-lite flexibility is fueling aggressive sponsor maneuvers: uptiering exchanges (priming minority lenders) and J.Crew-style intellectual property drop-downs into unrestricted subsidiaries. Non-participating BSL holders face recovery haircuts exceeding 35%.

CLO ENGINE ARBITRAGE

CLO liability spreads hold firm (AAA @ SOFR+135 bps). However, loan spread compression reduces the collateral pool's Weighted Average Spread (WAS), depressing CLO equity cash distributions from 14.5% to 10.8% as 2027 maturity walls loom.
