### Executive Alert: Leslie's Poolmart Downgraded to 'CCC-' (Restructuring Imminent)

* **Downgrade to 'CCC-' on Restructuring Risk**: [S&P Global Ratings](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3615908) downgraded **Leslie's Poolmart Inc. to 'CCC-' from 'CCC'** with a Negative Outlook on August 21, 2026. The rating action reflects an elevated probability of a distressed debt exchange or out-of-court balance sheet restructuring over the next six months before its **$756 million senior secured term loan** becomes current in March 2027 (due March 2028).
* **Revenue Contraction & Withdrawn Guidance**: Following weaker-than-expected fiscal Q3 results (revenue -8.4% YoY, comparable same-store sales -6.2%), the company withdrew full-year 2026 guidance ([Investing.com Coverage](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/sp-cuts-leslies-rating-on-restructuring-risk-93CH-4872063)). S&P projects fiscal 2026 revenue will decline ~7%, pressured by lower consumer discretionary traffic, promotional discounting, and 80 store closures (reducing footprint to 943 locations).
* **Springing ABL Maturity & Liquidity Constraints**: While reported liquidity stands at $207 million, S&P expects to exclude availability under the $250 million Asset-Based Lending (ABL) facility from its liquidity assessment once the ABL approaches its springing maturity in December 2026/2027 ahead of the unrefinanced term loan ([S&P Research Update](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3615907)).
* **Cash Flow Deficits & 9.9x Leverage**: S&P forecasts a Free Operating Cash Flow (FOCF) deficit of **~$19 million in FY2026**, with adjusted leverage projected at **9.9x in FY2026** and **8.7x in FY2027**, alongside thin Funds From Operations (FFO) to debt of only 2.8%.

### 2nd Line of Defense (2LoD) Risk Implications

1. **Springing Maturity Trigger Audits on Retail ABLs**:
* Audit all asset-based lending (ABL) facilities extended to leveraged retail and consumer borrowers, verifying springing maturity clauses that accelerate revolver expirations 90–120 days prior to term loan maturity dates ([S&P Global Research Update](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3615907)).


2. **Pre-Restructuring Valuation & Recovery Modeling**:
* Re-evaluate recovery assumptions for 'CCC' and 'CCC-' retail term loans trading below par. Model enterprise emergence valuations using stressed **4.5x–5.0x retail EBITDA multiples** ([Investing.com Coverage](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/sp-cuts-leslies-rating-on-restructuring-risk-93CH-4872063)), applying strict collateral haircuts for inventory liquidations.


3. **Early Identification of "Zombie" Retail Turnarounds**:
* Flag sponsor-backed consumer specialty retailers generating persistent negative free operating cash flow (FOCF) where cost-cutting and store consolidation fail to offset top-line traffic declines ([S&P Global Rating Action](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3615908)).
