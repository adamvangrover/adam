## Module 4: BSL Market Update & The Repricing Mirage

The Broadly Syndicated Loan (BSL) market is experiencing a massive repricing wave driven by intense institutional demand for yield and robust collateralized loan obligation (CLO) creation. Spreads on BB and B-rated tranche loans have compressed aggressively, often tightening by 25–50 basis points on opportunistic refinancing transactions.

### The Private Credit Convergence

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  BSL vs. Private Credit Dynamics                   |
+----------------------+--------------------+-----------------------+
| Metric               | BSL Market         | Private Credit        |
+----------------------+--------------------+-----------------------+
| Average Spread       | SOFR + 275-325 bps | SOFR + 500-600 bps    |
| Covenant Structure   | Covenant-Lite      | Financial Covenants   |
| Liquidity Profile    | Secondary Trading  | Illiquid / Buy & Hold |
| Refinancing Velocity | Rapid / Repricings | Slower / Bilateral    |
+----------------------+--------------------+-----------------------+

```

This yield compression has eroded the historical premium that BSL offered over risk-adjusted benchmarks, creating a **"Repricing Mirage."** While borrower interest expense drops in the short term, loan structures are being stripped of maintenance covenants, leaving lenders vulnerable to cliff-risk losses in a downside event.

### Historical Perspective

Current market behavior mirrors late-cycle dynamics observed in **2007** and **2018**, where aggressive spread compression and the proliferation of "cov-lite" structures signaled a market prioritizing deal velocity over risk discrimination. As in prior cycles, compressed spreads today may not adequately compensate for ultimate Loss Given Default (LGD) risks tomorrow.
