## Module 4: BSL Market Update & The Repricing Mirage

### Syndicated Credit Dynamics

The Broadly Syndicated Loan (BSL) market is experiencing significant spread tightening, driven by an abundance of institutional capital chasing limited net-new primary issuance. Borrowers are capitalizing on this technical imbalance to execute aggressive repricing transactions, stripping out yield while rolling over existing maturities.

| Metric / Dimension | Broadly Syndicated Loans (BSL) | Private Credit Direct Lending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary Pricing / Spreads** | SOFR + 275–325 bps | SOFR + 500–600 bps |
| **Documentation / Covenants** | Covenant-lite structures dominate | Financial maintenance covenants standard |
| **Liquidity & Secondary Market** | High secondary market turnover | Buy-and-hold; limited liquidity |
| **Repricing Velocity** | Extremely rapid exercise of repricing options | Lower repricing pressure; negotiated flex |

### Historical Context & Late-Cycle Patterns

Current behavior mirrors classic late-cycle credit market dynamics last observed during pre-crisis credit expansions. The reliance on covenant-lite structures and aggressively adjusted EBITDA definitions creates a "Repricing Mirage"—where nominal credit spreads appear tight and defaults artificially suppressed, yet true recovery values (LGD) are systematically degraded.

## Module 5: Counterfactual Scenario Analysis

### Stress Test Scenario: *Surprise Energy-Driven Rate Hike (+50 bps)*

An unexpected geopolitical energy shock drives headline inflation higher, forcing central banks to enact a surprise 50 bps policy rate hike.

* **1. Credit Risk Impact:**
* **Coverage Ratios:** Fixed-charge coverage ratios (FCCR) across B- and CCC-rated borrowers fall below 1.0x.
* **PD Shifts:** Probability of Default (PD) curves shift sharply upward over a 12-month forward horizon.


* **2. Market Risk Impact:**
* **Valuation Repricing:** Bond and loan portfolios experience immediate mark-to-market drawdowns as discount rates surge.
* **Spread Widening:** Credit risk premia widen abruptly, reversing recent tight BSL market repricings.


* **3. Liquidity Risk Impact:**
* **Primary Market Freeze:** New issue market stalls, preventing lower-tier corporates from refinancing near-term maturities.
* **Drawdowns:** Borrowers aggressively draw down on revolving credit facilities to satisfy short-term working capital needs.

## Module 7: Behavioral Finance Corner

### The Trap: Anchoring Bias

Risk teams and credit committees frequently anchor their forward-looking loss expectations to historically low default rates observed during recent low-interest rate regimes.

> **Rule of Thumb for Risk Managers:** > Never evaluate debt service capacity using trailing 3-year historical average interest rates; always stress-test borrower cash flows against current SOFR plus a minimum **+150 bps upward sensitivity overlay**.
