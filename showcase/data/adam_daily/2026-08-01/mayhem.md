## 3. MACRO & POLICY OUTLOOK: THE 4.75% YIELD ANCHOR

The fundamental architecture of the global macro-economy is currently defined by the **4.75% yield** on the 10-year US Treasury note.

### 3.1 Yield Curve Dynamics: The Bear Steepening Regime

The 10-year to 2-year yield spread stands at +47 bps (4.75% vs. 4.28%), reflecting a classic **"bear steepening"** regime. The current environment is driven not by impending monetary easing, but rather by term-premium expansion and fierce supply-absorption dynamics. Higher real yields at the long end of the curve are effectively doing a portion of the Fed's tightening work.

### 3.2 The Adrian-Crump-Moench (ACM) Model & Term Premium Expansion

The ACM model indicates that the 10-year term premium has breached positive territory and expanded significantly. This is driven by:

1. **Fiscal supply absorption:** Sovereign borrowing needs have expanded exponentially.
2. **Inflation risk premium:** Elevated commodity inputs and deglobalization force investors to demand higher compensation.
3. **Demographic shifts:** The transition of the post-war generation from "super-savers" to "super-consumers" reduces the structural demand for government debt.

### 3.3 Equity Risk Premium (ERP) Compression

With the S&P 500 aggregate earnings yield hovering near 4.00% to 4.75%, subtracting the 4.75% 10-year Treasury yield results in an **ERP that is functionally zero**, or in some instances, inverted. Current equity valuations are therefore exquisitely sensitive to any deterioration in forward earnings estimates.

## 4. CORPORATE CREDIT & LEVERAGED FINANCE

As the baseline risk-free rate anchors near 5.00%, corporate credit markets are undergoing a precarious recalibration against an imposing "maturity wall" entering 2026 and 2027.

### 4.1 BSL vs. Private Credit Dynamics

* **Broadly Syndicated Loans (BSL):** Primary syndication activity is concentrated in high-quality BB and B1 tranches, clearing near SOFR + 325 to 350 bps. Lower-tier issuers (B3 and CCC) face severe refinancing resistance.
* **Private Credit:** Direct lenders demand SOFR + 550 to 600 bps with strict maintenance covenants for LBO transactions. The absolute interest burden placed on portfolio companies remains punitive.

### 4.2 ICR and DSCR Compression

Interest Coverage Ratios (ICRs) across B3 and CCC-rated cohorts are compressing rapidly toward the critical 1.0x threshold. An ICR below 1.0x signifies a firm cannot service its debt via organic cash flow.

| Sector / Asset Class | Minimum DSCR Threshold | Risk Profile and Sensitivities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operational Renewable Energy** | 1.20x – 1.35x | Moderate; highly sensitive to PPA pricing and grid curtailment. |
| **Infrastructure PPP (Availability)** | 1.15x – 1.30x | Low; backed by sovereign or municipal availability payments. |
| **Commercial Real Estate / Data Centers** | 1.25x – 1.50x | High; exposed to lease roll-over, tenant credit risk, and refinancing shocks. |
| **Mining and Resources** | 1.10x – 1.25x | High; exposed to extreme commodity price volatility and geopolitical friction. |

### 4.3 CLO Mechanics and Securitization Vulnerabilities

CLO structures rely on strict mathematical tests to protect senior tranche investors, primarily the **Overcollateralization (OC) test**. If a CLO fails its OC test due to widespread B3/CCC distress, structural cash flow diversion is automatically triggered, destroying yield for CLO equity investors and freezing new CLO formation.

### 4.4 Liability Management Exercises (LMEs): "Creditor-on-Creditor Violence"

Faced with maturity walls, borrowers are deploying aggressive LMEs to avoid Chapter 11. Over 90% of leveraged loans are "covenant-lite," allowing borrowers to exploit loopholes:

* **Non-Pro Rata Uptier Exchange:** Distressed borrowers work with a simple majority of lenders to strip negative covenants and create a "super-priority" debt tranche, subordinating minority lenders.
* **Drop-Down Transaction:** Borrowers transfer valuable collateral (IP, real estate) into "unrestricted subsidiaries" to raise fresh debt, stripping legacy lenders of collateral backing.
