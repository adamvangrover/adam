## Known gaps

- No live market or fundamentals feed: every figure comes from the seeded path `data/scenarios/2026H2_path.json` and the illustrative inputs in `config/valuation/coverage_2026.yaml`.
- Credit memo and deep dive families have templates and schema support but no backfilled editions.

- Market Mayhem weeks with an existing `newsletters/market_mayhem_2026*.md` narrative link it as unverified; older 2025–early-2026 hand-made pages are not converted to schema records.
- Reviews are deterministic and offline; the LLM desk / red-team / judge path is off (`config/publication_review.yaml`).
