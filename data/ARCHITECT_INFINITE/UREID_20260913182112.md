### 3.3 Quantitative Risk Models & Formalisms

#### A. Continuous-Time Markov Chain PD Migration Engine

Credit migration across rating states $S = \{\text{AAA}, \text{AA}, \text{A}, \text{BBB}, \text{BB}, \text{B}, \text{CCC}, \text{D}\}$ is modeled as a continuous-time Markov process governed by the infinitesimal generator matrix $Q$:

$$Q = \begin{pmatrix} q_{11} & q_{12} & \cdots & q_{1k} \\ q_{21} & q_{22} & \cdots & q_{2k} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

Where $q_{ij} \ge 0$ for $i \ne j$, and diagonal elements satisfy:

$$q_{ii} = -\sum_{j \ne i} q_{ij}$$

The transition probability matrix $P(t)$ for horizon $t$ is computed using the matrix exponential:

$$P(t) = \exp(Q t) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(Q t)^k}{k!}$$

Under a simulated macroeconomic shock scenario (+150 bps rate curve shift + 15% revenue contraction), the generator matrix transforms via stress operator $\Phi(\Delta r, \Delta \text{Rev})$:

$$q_{ij}^{\text{stressed}} = q_{ij}^{\text{base}} \cdot \exp\left( \beta_1 \Delta r + \beta_2 \Delta \text{Rev} \right), \quad \forall j > i$$

This captures the acceleration of downgrade transitions in the lower tranches of the portfolio.

#### B. Dynamic Backlog-Adjusted Leverage Architecture

For growth-oriented defense and software firms with non-linear contract cycles, leverage headroom is calculated by discounting firm backlog obligations ($B_k$) by execution probability ($\alpha_k$):

$$\text{Headroom} = C_{\text{Max}} - \left[ \frac{D_{\text{Total}} - \text{Cash}_{\text{Unrestricted}}}{R_{\text{ARR}} + \sum_{k=1}^{N} (\alpha_k \cdot B_k \cdot \delta_k)} \right]$$

Where:

* $C_{\text{Max}}$: Maximum permitted leverage multiple under the credit agreement.
* $D_{\text{Total}}$: Total funded debt.
* $R_{\text{ARR}}$: Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue.
* $\alpha_k \in [0, 1]$: Historical appropriation rate for defense program line item $k$.
* $\delta_k$: Discount factor reflecting delivery milestone schedules.
